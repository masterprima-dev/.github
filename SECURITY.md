# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public issues, PRs, or discussions.**

Use GitHub's private reporting instead: open the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**.

If that option isn't available on the repo, contact the organization owners privately and share details only through a private channel.

Please include:

- What is affected (repo, component, URL).
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The impact you think it has.

## What to expect

We'll acknowledge the report, investigate, and keep you updated. Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before any public disclosure.

## Scope

Anything in this organization's repositories. For problems with the public website or student-facing services, report them the same way.

Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data that isn't yours while testing, and never test against real student or customer accounts.
