# Code of Conduct

We want Masterprima's engineering spaces (repos, issues, PRs, discussions) to be respectful and productive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, background, and experience level.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or insults of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and PRs, and may restrict or remove contributors who violate this code.

To report a violation, contact the organization owners privately (for example via a direct message), not in a public thread.
